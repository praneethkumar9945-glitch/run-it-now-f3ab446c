# Mobile homepage refinement

## What will change
- Rework the top feature area so its headline, person image, controls, and action buttons fit within a phone screen without overlap.
- Tighten section spacing and headings for easier mobile scanning.
- Keep multi-item event sections in compact two-column or horizontal swipe layouts where appropriate.
- Make story, community, newsletter, and footer layouts readable and touch-friendly on narrow screens.
- Preserve the current desktop design and content.

## Validation
- Check the complete homepage at phone and desktop sizes.
- Confirm no sideways page overflow, clipped text, overlapping controls, or broken navigation.
